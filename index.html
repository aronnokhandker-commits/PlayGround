Welcome to Moonroot

By daylight, grow a garden. After dusk, protect it from the hungry things beyond the hedgerow.

  • Plant carrots and moonberries in the furrows. Water crops each day to help them grow.
  • Build pea turrets on the stone pads. They'll automatically pelt anything on the path.
  • Harvest ripe crops for gold, then survive 5 nights. Don't let the homestead fall!
